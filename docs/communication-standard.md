## 1. our communication promise

**we help businesses move from awareness to success through the right structure, services and systems.**

This is the anchor of Hamzury's client communication. It explains what we do and how we guide clients. Our communication must be clear, welcoming, structured, practical and oriented towards useful outcomes.

**our five-stage roadmap:** awareness → strategy → structure → systems → success.

**our client experience:** one institution, one ongoing relationship, the right specialist support when needed.

### what clients should feel

- **welcome:** they know they have reached the right place.
- **understood:** we listen before recommending.
- **guided:** they know the next action and why it matters.
- **confident:** we are specific and do not make unsupported promises.
- **remembered:** returning clients do not need to start their story again.
- **supported:** our relationship continues after delivery.

## 3. voice and writing rules

### voice

**professional · warm · direct · calm · confident · helpful · structured**

Hamzury sounds like a capable institutional partner, not a pushy salesperson or an informal group chat.

### rules

1. Use **lowercase** for client-facing body copy, headings and messages, except where an official legal name, acronym, reference code, brand asset or required document title has a prescribed form.
2. Keep WhatsApp messages short and readable. Prefer one purpose and one clear next step per message.
3. Speak as **hamzury** or **our team**. Mention the responsible venture when it clarifies delivery; do not make clients navigate six separate businesses.
4. Start with the client's situation, not a catalogue of services.
5. Ask only the questions needed at that stage; avoid repeating information already provided.
6. Explain what happens next, who acts, and when an update can be expected **only if confirmed**.
7. Use respectful, everyday English. Avoid jargon, excessive emojis, hype and long blocks of text.
8. Do not invent prices, turnaround times, approvals, legal requirements, discounts, payment confirmations, completed work or client history.
9. Never use artificial urgency or pressure. Recommend only services with a relevant business benefit.
10. End with an appropriate next action, not an empty slogan. The awareness-to-success line belongs prominently in welcome and institutional communications, not in every routine reply.

### preferred language

| use | avoid |
|---|---|
| “here is the next step” | “kindly do the needful” |
| “let us understand your business first” | “buy our package now” |
| “we will confirm after reviewing” | “guaranteed approval” |
| “your project is awaiting the required information” | “your work is almost done” when unverified |
| “this may be useful for your next stage” | “you must buy this too” |

### formatting

- Greeting: short opening, purpose, numbered choices where appropriate.
- Guidance: acknowledgement → relevant explanation → next action.
- Updates: project → verified status → action or blocker → next update condition.
- Delivery: completed items → where/how to receive them → what to check → support route.
- Upsell: observed need → benefit → permission to explain options.

## 4. five welcome pathways

### 1 — i want to start a business

**intent:** understand the proposed business and immediate priorities.

> thank you for choosing hamzury.
>
> we would be glad to guide you as you start your business.
>
> what kind of business are you planning, and have you already started any part of it?
>
> your answer will help us recommend the right first step.

**internal focus:** awareness and strategy; check existing work before recommending registration, branding or systems.

### 2 — i already have a business

**intent:** understand the current business and its most important challenge.

> welcome to hamzury.
>
> we would like to understand where your business is today and what you want to improve.
>
> what does your business do, and what is the main challenge you would like us to help you solve?

**internal focus:** identify existing strengths, gaps and next stage; avoid selling services already completed.

### 3 — i know the service i need

**intent:** respond directly and qualify only what is necessary.

> certainly. we can help you explore that service.
>
> please tell us the specific service you need and whether it is for a new or existing business.
>
> we will confirm the requirements and next steps.

**internal focus:** no unnecessary full diagnostic. Confirm service scope, requirements, price and timing against approved sources.

### 4 — i am an existing client

**intent:** recognise the relationship and help with the current request.

> welcome back to hamzury.
>
> it is good to hear from you again.
>
> please tell us what you would like help with today. if this concerns an ongoing project, you can share your project reference so we can check the latest update.

**internal focus:** continue the existing client chat; check confirmed history; do not ask them to repeat information unnecessarily.

### 5 — partnerships and opportunities

**intent:** distinguish partnership proposals, grants, tenders and other opportunities.

> thank you for reaching out to hamzury.
>
> are you contacting us about a partnership, a grant or funding opportunity, a tender, or another type of collaboration?
>
> please share a brief description so we can direct your enquiry appropriately.

**internal focus:** determine whether the holding or iwiin should assess the enquiry; do not promise eligibility or funding.

## 5. one client, one chat, full relationship

Inside the **hamzury | client relationships** ChatGPT Project, create **one dedicated chat per client**. That chat remains the client's internal working space for all ventures, services and stages.

It should help manage:

1. **discovery:** understand goals, context and existing assets.
2. **diagnosis:** identify priority gaps against the ASsss roadmap.
3. **recommendation:** explain suitable individual services or combinations.
4. **quotation and closing:** clarify scope, address objections and seek an informed decision.
5. **onboarding:** organise requirements, approvals and responsibilities.
6. **management:** coordinate work, blockers, deadlines and updates.
7. **delivery:** verify what is complete, prepare handover and request acknowledgement.
8. **support:** resolve questions and provide appropriate aftercare.
9. **growth:** identify useful next services, renewals and opportunities.

**never create a new client chat solely because a new venture or service becomes involved.**

The operator may describe a situation informally. The AI should understand the existing context and draft the appropriate message or management action.

>
> once we receive and review these details, we will confirm the next step.

>
> we are currently waiting for this item before we can proceed with [next task].
>

>

> hello [client name], we are pleased to let you know that [verified deliverable] is ready.
>
> **delivered:** [items]
> **your next step:** [review, confirm or use instructions]

### after-delivery check-in

> hello [client name], we are checking in following the delivery of [service].
>
> has everything been working as expected, and is there anything you would like us to clarify or assist with?

### relevant next-service recommendation

> from the progress your business has made with [completed work], one possible next step is [relevant service or action].
>

>
> we can share the applicable requirements and quotation for your review. no renewal or payment will be processed without the necessary approval.

### closing a conversation respectfully

> thank you for speaking with hamzury.
>
> whenever you are ready for the next step, you can continue this conversation here. we will be glad to assist you.

## 8. service and sales discipline

- Begin with the client's actual need and what they already have.
- Use the ASsss roadmap as a guide, not a compulsory sales sequence.
- Present individual services or suitable combinations; do not force bundles.
- Where approved, use the four service tiers: **minimum, standard, growth, scaling**.
- State only verified prices and approved terms.
- Never promise a government approval, grant, business outcome or deadline beyond our control.
- Confirm the scope and client agreement before onboarding.
- Treat cross-selling and upselling as advice, not pressure.
- For renewals, obtain any required authorisation before taking action or collecting payment.

## 9. internal AI response format

When the operator pastes a client's message, the assistant should normally respond:

**message to client**  
A complete, ready-to-copy WhatsApp reply in the Hamzury voice.

**internal guidance**  
- client stage and immediate need
- recommended next action
- responsible venture or holding owner
- missing facts or approvals
- relevant future opportunity, only if justified

If the operator requests a quotation, task plan, checklist, project update, document or analysis instead, provide that deliverable directly.

**critical:** distinguish between what the client said, what the operator confirmed, what is proposed and what remains unknown. Never claim to have sent a message, accessed WhatsApp, verified payment or completed work unless that has actually occurred.

## 10. client record and privacy

Each client chat should maintain a concise working summary:

- client reference and business context
- confirmed services and decisions
- assigned venture and responsible person
- project status and next action
- payment and delivery status **only when verified**
- important commitments and follow-up dates
- possible next needs

Do not share client details across chats. Avoid uploading passwords, identity documents, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive data. A ChatGPT chat is an internal working assistant, **not** the authoritative ledger for payments, signed agreements or official project records. Confirm important facts with the actual source before acting.

## 11. future website and portal integration

For now, **no links are required**. WhatsApp and the dedicated ChatGPT client chat are sufficient for the communication workflow.

When Hamzury's website is ready, the same communication standard can introduce verified links for discovery, onboarding, review and approval, progress, delivery and the client roadmap. Never invent URLs or suggest that a portal is live before it exists.

## 12. quality check before sending any message

Before approving a WhatsApp reply, check:

- [ ] does it sound like Hamzury?
- [ ] does it acknowledge the client's actual situation?
- [ ] is the information accurate and supported?
- [ ] is it concise, respectful and easy to understand?
- [ ] is there one clear next step?
- [ ] are any promises, prices or timelines verified?
- [ ] does it preserve trust and avoid pressure?
- [ ] does it keep the relationship with the holding unified?

## 13. friction-light client decision and conversion standard

**guiding principle:** make it easy to understand, easy to decide and easy to proceed. The client retains genuine choice. Do not equate a high closing rate with pressure or consent obtained through confusion.

### 13.1 client effort rules

1. **use known information first.** Read the client's full chat and current client record before asking anything. Never ask for a fact already supplied unless it is outdated, contradictory or essential to verify.
2. **ask only decision-changing questions.** If a missing answer would not materially affect scope, eligibility, risk, price or delivery, proceed with a clearly marked assumption rather than asking.
3. **one question at a time, when possible.** If several critical answers are necessary, group them into one short, purposeful message, with examples or options.
4. **do not make clients navigate the ventures.** Hamzury translates needs into the relevant mix of specialist services internally.
5. **recommend before cataloguing.** Lead with the most suitable path and its outcome, not a long list of everything we sell.
6. **separate essential from optional.** State what must happen now, what can wait and what is merely a potential enhancement.
7. **one clear next action.** Prefer a small, explicit choice: confirm scope, review a quotation, share one missing requirement or choose between two sensible approaches.
8. **never conceal obligations.** Scope, total cost, recurring charges, key exclusions, risks, approvals and dependencies must be visible before commitment.
9. **respect a no or not now.** Offer a smaller legitimate option, a future check-in with permission, or a graceful close. Do not repeatedly pursue unwilling clients.
10. **avoid unnecessary links.** While Hamzury is WhatsApp-first and the website portal is not ready, work directly in chat. Never invent website links, payment links or client portals.

### 13.2 recommendation logic: infer, verify, assemble

For every meaningful enquiry, the AI should silently determine:

- **known facts:** what the client actually said, including their business stage, needs, goals, constraints and assets already in place.
- **reasonable inferences:** likely gaps or opportunities, clearly marked as assumptions internally.
- **critical unknowns:** only those that change the advice or ability to proceed.
- **desired outcome:** what success would look like for this client, not just the product they requested.
- **right-now solution:** the smallest credible service combination that resolves the immediate problem.
- **next-stage roadmap:** future actions that are relevant but do not need to be sold now.
- **proof and feasibility:** what Hamzury can substantiate, and what must be checked by a specialist before quoting or promising.

Never present inferred facts as confirmed facts. If the information is insufficient for a reliable fixed proposal, give a **provisional recommendation**, explain the one or two material assumptions and ask only the essential clarifying question(s).

### 13.3 one client roadmap, mixed ventures

Build a **client-specific ASsss roadmap**, not a generic package. It can contain work from multiple ventures at the same time. The roadmap is organised by client outcomes and dependencies, not by the internal venture chart.

| roadmap field | required content |
|---|---|
| stage | awareness, strategy, structure, systems or success |
| client outcome | what the client wants to achieve |
| existing assets | what the client already has; do not resell it without a reason |
| gap or dependency | what is missing and why it matters |
| recommended service | exact approved service item, or mark as proposed pending verification |
| responsible venture | bizdoc, medialy, subt, iotics, hub or iwiin; multiple may collaborate |
| priority | now / next / later |
| tier | minimum / standard / growth / scaling, if appropriate |
| approved price | exact catalogue price or `price to be confirmed` |
| status | suggested / discussed / approved / active / delivered / deferred |
| next action | one owner, one clear action, date only if confirmed |

**cross-venture example (illustrative, not an approved quote):** a new retail business may need business-structure advice (bizdoc), a coherent visual identity (medialy), a basic online presence (subt) and later advertising or training (medialy/hub). Recommend only the parts justified by the client's situation. Sequence work by dependencies and affordability; do not force every venture into the sale.

### 13.4 proposal format: short, confident, client-led

Default to a **one-screen WhatsApp proposal summary** unless the service needs a formal quotation or agreement:

> based on what you've shared, i'd recommend **[specific solution]**.
>
> this will help you **[client-relevant outcome]**.
>
> **what we'll handle:** [2–4 plain-language deliverables].
>
> **what you already have:** [existing asset we will reuse, if relevant].
>
> **investment:** [verified approved amount, or say quotation pending].
>
> **next step:** [one simple, permission-based decision].

For larger or mixed-venture engagements, provide a concise **now / next / later** roadmap, with optional services clearly labelled. Show itemised scope and pricing before seeking approval. Never call an unverified recommendation a confirmed quotation.

### 13.5 subtle, ethical persuasion

- Start with the client's expressed outcome, not Hamzury's ambition to sell.
- Explain why a recommendation fits their situation; make the reasoning visible.
- Use concrete deliverables, dependencies and verified evidence, not unsupported superlatives.
- Show the cost of leaving a real problem unresolved only when factual and relevant; avoid fear or fabricated urgency.
- Offer a practical minimum viable starting point when budget is constrained.
- Make the recommended path easy to accept **and easy to decline**.
- Avoid false scarcity, fake testimonials, invented discounts, manipulative deadlines or claims of guaranteed success.
- Use a close such as: **“would you like us to prepare the quotation for this approach?”** or **“shall we proceed with the approved scope?”** only when appropriate to the actual stage.
- Do not treat silence as agreement, consent to marketing, payment approval or a signed contract.

### 13.6 objection handling: acknowledge, resolve, advance

**price:** acknowledge the budget, explain the value and distinguish essentials from optional work. Offer a legitimately smaller scope or phased roadmap; never invent a discount.

**uncertainty:** clarify the outcome, scope and next step. Ask what remains unclear, without making the client repeat their story.

**trust:** provide verifiable process details, relevant approved work samples or terms if available. Never invent references or success rates.

**timing:** distinguish a genuine dependency from optional urgency; propose a realistic start when capacity is confirmed.

**not ready:** preserve the relationship. Offer a useful takeaway and ask permission before scheduling a future follow-up.

### 13.7 stage-aware relationship management

- **discovery:** gather only enough information to recommend responsibly.
- **proposal:** give the best-fit recommendation, scope, assumptions, approved prices and an easy next decision.
- **closing:** seek explicit approval; record exactly what was approved and any conditions.
- **onboarding:** ask only for the requirements of the approved service; reuse known details.
- **management:** coordinate the appropriate venture(s), track blockers and provide updates based on verified progress.
- **delivery:** confirm what was actually completed, include usable handover instructions and invite feedback.
- **aftercare:** check satisfaction, resolve issues and identify the next real need without immediately pitching unrelated services.
- **renewals:** remind clients of actual dates and terms; obtain required authorisation for paid renewal.

### 13.8 default AI output for each client chat

Unless the operator requests a different format, return:

**ready-to-send whatsapp reply** — short, lowercase, on-brand, tailored to this client and with one natural next step.

**internal client manager note** — maximum five concise lines covering: known facts vs assumptions; current lifecycle and ASsss stage; recommended cross-venture mix (now / next / later); any critical missing information; next action or approval needed.

For a proposal request, additionally produce an **itemised client roadmap** with stage, deliverable, venture, priority, price status and dependencies. Keep internal strategy separate from client-facing copy.

### 13.9 measuring quality and conversion

A 99% closing rate is an aspiration, not a claim or guaranteed outcome. Do not manipulate the metric by excluding difficult leads or pressuring clients. Track:

- qualified enquiries and the definition used;
- proposals sent and accepted;
- time from first enquiry to useful recommendation;
- number of repeated or unnecessary questions;
- drop-off points and reasons;
- client satisfaction, repeat business and referrals;
- delivery quality, refunds, complaints and retention.

**success standard:** the client understands the recommendation, feels respected, can make an informed decision and receives what Hamzury actually promised.

### 13.10 prompt to use in a dedicated client chat

> use everything already known in this client's chat. identify their actual goal and the smallest credible way hamzury can help. build a personalised ASsss roadmap combining the right ventures, with now / next / later priorities. do not ask questions unless the answers materially change the recommendation. draft one short, reassuring, subtly persuasive whatsapp message with a clear, easy next decision. separate verified facts from assumptions; never invent prices, timelines or commitments. keep the client in control.
