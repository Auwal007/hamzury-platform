# conversion playbook: every stage sells, honestly

hamzury holding / platform v6 / 5 november 2026

the client and their business are the hero. we are the guide who makes the next step obvious. every screen and message does one job: help the client see their situation clearly, trust us, and take the next step themselves.

## the rules for every sentence

1. **lead with their outcome, not our service.** "never lose an order again", not "we build order management systems".
2. **use their words back to them.** reflections, the clarity snapshot and "you told us" all quote the client.
3. **one ask per screen and per message.** one button, one next step.
4. **remove risk out loud.** "you see every price first", "nothing starts until you choose", "about 1 minute".
5. **let them choose.** "build your plan", essential / recommended / optional, unlock when ready.
6. **never invent.** no fake reviews, numbers, deadlines, scarcity or guarantees. this is our brand promise and it is what earns the second purchase.

## the journey and what each stage must achieve

| stage | the client feels | what does the work |
|---|---|---|
| whatsapp hello | welcomed, not sold to | greeting message from the whatsapp kit with two links: help fast, or full picture |
| home | "they understand businesses like mine" | outcome-led hook, venture tiles that open in place, ai explained simply, risk removed |
| route choice | in control of their time | "i need help fast" (1 minute) or "show me the full picture" (4 minutes) |
| questions | understood | multi-choice with "other", their business name in every question, reflections in their words |
| clarity snapshot | "yes, that is us" | everything they said on one screen before contact details |
| id | safe and on a clear path | big id, what happens next, back to whatsapp |
| plan ready message | curious, respected | "your plan for ada foods is ready. you choose what we do." private link, no login |
| build your plan | in charge of the decision | "you told us", why each part fits them, fees visible, running total |
| payment | reassured | exact amount, reference, never-share-your-pin note |
| onboarding | it is moving | only the questions for what they bought, about 2 minutes |
| progress | calm | progress bars, updates within 48 hours, download when delivered |
| next step | ready for more | "you have finished x. the next step is y", unlock button, follow-up message |

## the daily sales rhythm (desk lead, 15 minutes)

1. open **follow-ups**: message every urgent request first, then unpaid choices, then missing onboarding.
2. publish every plan that is due. send the "plan ready" message straight away, while they are still thinking about it.
3. after each delivery, send the "next step" message within a day.

## what to measure every friday (insights)

- **finished to paid**: the single number that says whether the journey converts.
- **where people stop**: if one screen loses many people, shorten or reword it first.
- **hours to publish a plan**: a fast plan keeps the client's momentum. aim to beat the 48-hour promise, then check in insights whether faster plans pay more often.
- **bought again**: proof that clients trust the roadmap.

## what to add next (needs your decision)

- **real proof**: short client stories and results, only with written permission. add them to the home page and the plan page.
- **whatsapp cloud api**: send the ready messages from the desk automatically once volumes justify it, still reviewed by a person.
- **a/b testing of the hook**: once traffic is steady, test two headlines for two weeks and keep the winner.
