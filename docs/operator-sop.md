# hamzury holding | operating SOP for the client desk

## core rule

**one client · one relationship · one integrated roadmap · one accountable holding experience.** The six ventures are delivery capabilities, not six new client journeys.

### 1 — first enquiry

1. Apply the approved five-option welcome message when appropriate.
2. Review all information already given. Ask only a question that materially changes scope, cost or legal requirements.
3. Create a single client record with the lead's identity and goal; do not collect unnecessary sensitive information.
4. Start/continue their **dedicated ChatGPT chat** in `hamzury | client relationships`. Do not make a second ChatGPT chat because they need another service.
5. Record the next best action as a concrete, verifiable step.

### 2 — diagnosis and proposal

1. Understand the outcome sought and what has already been completed. Do not sell registration to a client who is already registered unless there is a real additional need.
2. Assemble the minimum viable solution across appropriate ventures; include only useful dependencies.
3. Use the ASsss roadmap to prioritise **now / next / later**, but do not require the client to purchase every phase.
4. Prepare one client-specific proposal. Include clear inclusions, exclusions, authorised naira prices, relevant third-party costs where confirmed, and verified timeframe.
5. Review and mark the proposal ready, then generate the secure client proposal link.
6. Send the link **manually via WhatsApp**. The private URL is shown once. Any indication to proceed must be separately confirmed by holding.

### 3 — onboarding and execution

1. Confirm the client's approved scope and any applicable payment terms through authoritative records. Do not equate a proposal-link click with a paid/contracted project.
2. Create/approve project tasks with the correct ventures. A service can involve multiple separate venture projects.
3. Send a structured onboarding/clarification link only for genuinely missing information.
4. Venture teams update their status and next step. They do not contact the client or alter commercial scope without holding approval.
5. Holding consolidates updates into one client-friendly message when multiple ventures work together.

### 4 — verification and delivery

1. Venture submits completed work for holding review.
2. Holding either requests corrections or approves QA.
3. Only after the deliverables have actually been transferred/communicated to the client does holding record **delivered**.
4. Client acceptance, client viewing a page, holding delivery and verified payment remain different events.
5. Deliver instructions for use, expected support path, and an aftercare touchpoint.

### 5 — retention and ethical next service

1. Follow up after delivery; resolve complaints before discussing another sale.
2. Review the roadmap and suggest the smallest next valuable step only when supported by the client's situation.
3. Explain the value and trade-offs, let the client decide, then document the agreed next step.

### 6 — AI assistant output contract (manual)

In each client ChatGPT chat request: **(a)** the WhatsApp message to send (lower-case, short, warm and branded) and **(b)** internal next-best-action guidance (verified facts, known gaps, relevant ventures, client effort, commercial and delivery risks). Always manually check AI-generated pricing, dates, commitments and compliance claims against authoritative records before sending.

### 7 — privacy and security

Never paste passwords, government IDs, full bank information or other unnecessary sensitive data into client chats or bearer-link pages. Limit project visibility to assigned staff. Revoke a link when incorrectly issued or no longer needed. Escalate privacy, contractual or security incidents to authorised holding leadership.
