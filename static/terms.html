how we work together.

these terms, your accepted proposal and this privacy note make one agreement. your proposal lists exactly what you chose. where they differ, your proposal wins for that service. draft for legal review.

1. who you work with

you contract with the company named on your proposal and receipt. it coordinates every hamzury team (venture) working on your plan, and stays responsible to you for the whole plan. if a venture ever contracts with you separately, your proposal will say so clearly.

2. what you get

each service in your proposal states what is included, what is not included, how many revisions you get, the time it takes and what you receive. anything outside that is a change (see 6).

3. fees, vat and payment

you see the professional fee, any outside charges, any vat that applies and the total before you pay. one-off services are paid in full before work starts. outside charges (for example government fees) are shown separately and paid directly by you unless your proposal says otherwise. monthly services show the monthly fee and the billing date.

4. when work starts and how long it takes

work starts when we have your payment and the details or documents your service asks for. the time shown is our working time. where an agency or third party must approve something (for example cac, firs, the trademarks registry, licence offices, nsitf, pencom or bpp), their time is theirs and is shown separately where we know it. if we are waiting on you, the schedule for that service pauses until we have what we need.

5. your part

give us accurate information, answer our questions, and review what we send within a reasonable time. you confirm you have the right to share any material you give us.

6. changes

if you want something outside the agreed scope, we send a revised quote. we do not start the extra work until you accept it.

7. monthly services

the first month is paid when you activate. after that, a card you choose is charged on the billing date, or we send an invoice if you pay by transfer. you can stop a monthly service any time; it runs to the end of the month already paid. if a payment stays unpaid for 7 days, the service pauses until it is paid. when a monthly service ends, we help you export or take over anything you need.

8. cancelling one-off services

you can cancel before a service is delivered. we refund what you paid, less the value of work already done and any outside costs we told you about and already committed on your behalf. if money reaches us for an order that was replaced or a service that had ended, we refund it.

9. ownership

once paid, what we make for you is yours. accounts we set up for you are set up in your name where possible, and at handover we record the login address, account email and who controls recovery. we never write passwords in our records; we send access through invitations or a password manager. for custom software, your proposal states ownership, any licences, hosting and maintenance separately. third-party tools stay under their own terms.

10. confidentiality

we keep your information and documents confidential and use them only to deliver your services. we never show your work in our portfolio without your written permission.

11. what we do not promise

we do our work with care and to the standard described. we cannot promise results that depend on others, such as approvals by agencies, decisions by funders, sales or rankings. our total responsibility for a service is limited to the fees you paid for that service, except where the law does not allow such a limit.

12. complaints and disputes

tell us first at desk@hamzury.com or on whatsapp. we reply within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 10 working days. if we cannot agree, we will try mediation before any court. these terms follow the laws of the federal republic of nigeria.

13. records

when you accept, we record which proposal and which version of these terms you accepted, and the date. if we change these terms, the version you accepted continues to apply to that order.
