privacy note · version 2026-11-07

how we handle your information.

what we collect. your answers on start here, your name, phone number and email, the services you choose, the details and documents your services need, payment references, and the files we deliver to you.

why. to prepare your recommendation, deliver what you pay for, keep you informed and keep the records the law asks of us. we do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

who can see it. authorised holding staff. venture staff see only the services their team is delivering for you. our finance team sees payment records, not your documents.

where it is kept. in our own business-controlled systems. you open your files by signing in to your dashboard; we do not use public links for your documents.

how long we keep it

  • enquiries that did not go ahead, and their uploads: removed after 90 days without activity.
  • documents you upload for a service, including identity documents: removed within 90 days after the work is delivered, unless we must keep them and tell you why.
  • files we deliver: available in your dashboard for 12 months after handover, or for as long as a monthly service is active. we remind you before they are removed. you should keep your own copy.
  • invoices, payments, contracts and required compliance records: kept for as long as the law requires.
  • backups: replaced on a regular cycle, so removed information also leaves our backups.

where the law or a dispute requires us to keep something, we keep it with restricted access until that ends.

your hamzury id. your id and phone number together open your dashboard. keep them private.

payments. online payments are handled by paystack. we never see or store your card details. if you pay a monthly service by card, paystack keeps a secure reference so the card can be charged on the billing date; you can stop this any time. we will never ask for your card pin, otp or banking password.

your choices. ask to see, correct, export or remove your information by writing to desk@hamzury.com. we reply within 2 working days.
